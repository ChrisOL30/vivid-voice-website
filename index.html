Everything you need to know about Vivid Voice

Vivid Voice goes beyond simple voice recording by providing personal prompts, organization, powerful search, and insights to help you track patterns in your thinking and behaviors. Additionally, our integrated goal tracking system helps you turn reflections into actions.

At launch, Vivid Voice will be available for iOS mobile devices. We're planning Android version shortly after launch, with all platforms syncing seamlessly through your secure account.

Vivid Voice is offered as a subscription billed monthly or annually. Payments are processed through your App Store account. If you cancel, your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period and you won't be charged again. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through App Store account settings.
